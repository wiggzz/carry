Client background requests

Costs, coverage & diagnostics

Savings compare the same recorded trajectory with a simulated conventional compaction policy, including completed reviewer costs. Where marked “successful requests only”, failed calls are excluded from both scenarios, including calls before later successes. The backend supplies session estimates and totals; unavailable comparisons have null amounts in the stats JSON and are excluded from totals. They are estimates, not measured savings or subscription charges; the observed priced subtotal includes all available receipts.
