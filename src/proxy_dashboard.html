How this is estimated

With Carry includes completed primary and reviewer costs. Traditional models the same recorded activity with conventional summaries. The difference is hypothetical; cache and summary assumptions can affect it even without a Carry rewrite. Costs cover the available accounting window, not necessarily the whole conversation.

Unavailable comparisons are excluded, not treated as zero. Failed or unpriced calls are excluded where noted; observed usage has separate cost coverage.
